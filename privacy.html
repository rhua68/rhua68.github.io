Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Fantasy Tool is a personal application used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

Google user data

The app requests the drive.file permission, which only gives access to files the app itself creates. It uses this solely to upload, list and delete its own backup files in one Google Drive folder ("fantasy-gm-backups"). It does not read, collect, share, sell or transfer any other Google data, and it does not use Google data for advertising or to train AI models.

Storage and retention

Backups are kept for 14 days and then deleted automatically. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
